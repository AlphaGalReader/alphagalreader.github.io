Privacy policy

Effective November 2, 2026. Alpha-Gal Rx is made by Histamine Health LLC, Missouri, USA.

The short version

You don’t create an account. We don’t run ads, use analytics or tracking, or sell or share your information. What you enter stays on your device, and we can’t see it.

Information stored only on your device

  • Your sensitivity level and other allergies you select.
  • Card details you enter: name, date of birth, allergist’s name and phone, emergency contact, and allergy notes.
  • Tracker entries: alpha-gal IgE results, reactions, meals, tick bites and their notes.
  • Photos you attach to meals or reactions, and your grocery list.

This information is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it. If you save a backup file, that file is yours: it goes wherever you choose to save or send it.

When the app uses the internet

  • Medication data. When you look up a medicine, the app downloads that medicine’s ingredient file from our data server, hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). The request names the medicine, not you. Like any web server, GitHub may log your device’s IP address under its own privacy statement.
  • Barcode lookups. When you scan or type a barcode, the barcode number may be sent to Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts and Open Products Facts, public product databases, to get the product’s ingredients. Skin and body products already in the app are checked on your phone.
  • Product search. When you type something the food guide doesn’t know, such as a brand name, the text you typed is sent to Open Food Facts to find matching products.
  • Recipe links. When you enter a recipe link, the app opens that web page, as a browser would, to read its ingredient list.
  • Links you tap, such as a drug label on DailyMed, open in your web browser.
  • Store links (“Where to buy”) open the store’s website in your browser, and the store’s own privacy policy applies there. Some links may include an affiliate code, which lets the store pay us a commission.

None of these requests are sent with your name, health information, or tracker entries.

Camera, photos and notifications

The camera is used only to scan barcodes, read ingredient labels and take photos you choose to attach. Label photos are read on your device and aren’t kept. Photos stay on your device. Reminders, such as tick-bite follow-ups, are scheduled on your device; we don’t send push notifications.

Children

The app is intended for adults and for parents or caregivers. It is not directed to children under 13.

Not a medical record

We are not your healthcare provider, and the app is not part of your medical record. Because your information never reaches us, we cannot recover it if your device is lost. Use the backup feature to keep a copy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
