Support

Questions, a medicine that looks wrong, or an idea? Email alphagalreader@gmail.com.

We can’t give individual medical advice. For questions about your own care, contact your allergist or pharmacist.

Where is my information stored?

Only on your phone. We have no account system and never receive your health information.

How do I move my information to a new phone?

In the app, go to Profile, then Backup & restore, and save a backup file to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email. On the new phone, install the app and choose Restore from a backup.

Why does a medicine say its ingredient list looks incomplete?

Some drug labels, often from repackagers, list only some ingredients. The app marks these instead of calling them fine. Ask your pharmacist about that product, or pick a manufacturer with a complete label.

Is this medical advice?

No. The app gives educational information from public drug labels and food data. Always confirm medicines with your pharmacist or prescriber, and follow your allergist’s advice.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. Cancel in your device’s account settings under Subscriptions.

Alpha-Gal Rx gives educational information, not medical advice. Drug labels and food data can be incomplete or out of date, and manufacturers change ingredients. Always confirm medicines with your pharmacist or prescriber. In an emergency, use your epinephrine and call 911.
