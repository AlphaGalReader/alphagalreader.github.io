Where the data comes from

Medication ingredients come from FDA drug labels published by the U.S. National Library of Medicine (DailyMed and RxNorm), refreshed monthly. Product data comes from Open Food Facts. Which ingredients are flagged, and how, was decided by a board-certified allergist.

Alpha-Gal Rx gives educational information, not medical advice. Drug labels and food data can be incomplete or out of date, and manufacturers change ingredients. Always confirm medicines with your pharmacist or prescriber. In an emergency, use your epinephrine and call 911.
